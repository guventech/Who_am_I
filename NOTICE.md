  scales per factor (`source: "ipip"`). Verified against
  https://ipip.ori.org/newBigFive5broadKey.htm (2026-10-20).
- **Honesty-Humility deeper items (HH01–HH06):** IPIP representations of
  HEXACO-PI Honesty-Humility facets (`source: "ipip-hexaco"`). Verified against
  https://ipip.ori.org/newhexaco_pi_key.htm (2026-10-20).
- **Cross-loader candidates (X01–X10):** original stems for this product
  (`source: "original"`); not from IPIP.
